Does an AI employee act without approval?

Only within the permissions and guardrails you approve for its role. External actions can require a person to review them before anything is sent or changed.

Can Nuumx finish an organisation I started?

Yes. On the hybrid path you hand your draft to an FDE, keep every choice you made, and get help with integrations or workflows.

What happens after I select a plan?

You set up your account and payment, connect knowledge and tools, test in a sandbox, and approve go-live as the owner.

Are these prices final?

Not yet. They are indicative starting points. Final pricing, included task credits, taxes and commercial terms are confirmed with you before purchase.
